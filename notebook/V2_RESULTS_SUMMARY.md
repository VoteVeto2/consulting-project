## 1. Data & Methodology

- **Frequency:** 24,774 policies, 49.6 % claim rate.
- **Severity:** 12,252 positive claims, median 563 EUR, max 12,878 EUR.
- **Approach:** Two-stage marginal decomposition — the canonical pricing
  pipeline when datasets are non-linkable. Dependent-frequency-severity
  models (copulas, Tweedie, Heckman) are structurally inapplicable without
  record-level linkage.

## 4. Validation & Conclusions

All 200 replications completed successfully.

| Tier | Mean LF | Std | 95 % CI |
|------|--------:|----:|--------:|
| Low | 0.580 | 0.009 | [0.564, 0.596] |
| Medium | 1.128 | 0.004 | [1.121, 1.136] |
| High | 1.673 | 0.015 | [1.643, 1.701] |
| Very High | 2.412 | 0.043 | [2.326, 2.495] |

The confidence intervals are tight -- the tier ordering is **stable across
resamples**, not a sampling artefact. Even the lower bound of the Very High
CI (2.33x) remains well above the action threshold of 2.0x.

### 4.2 Key Findings & Caveats

**Headline results:**

1. **GLM and GBM agree** on the overall risk ranking -- robust to model choice.
2. **Top decile** carries 21.4% of expected losses at LF = 2.14x.
3. **Unprofitable profile is coherent**: young (~23 y), male (~86%),
   unemployed (~62%), urban (~237 inh/km^2), no MD cover (~86%).
4. **Bootstrap CIs** confirm tier-level LFs are stable, not sampling artefacts.

**Honest caveats:**

1. **No premium data** -- profitability is portfolio-relative, not absolute.
2. **Independence assumption** (N indep. Y | x) is invoked, not tested.
   Garrido et al. (2016) show mean-prediction bias is typically modest;
   tail/variance bias can be larger.
3. **~12 k severity records** limit severity-side model complexity.
4. Bootstrap CIs quantify estimation uncertainty, not model-specification
   uncertainty.

**Methodology note:**

> *"The standard GLM tariff analysis is to do separate analyses for claim
> frequency and claim severity, and then relativities for the pure premium
> are found by multiplying the results."*
> -- Ohlsson & Johansson (2010)

The two-stage marginal decomposition is the canonical pricing pipeline, not a
workaround. The GBM benchmark confirms robustness (Gini 0.326 vs 0.297). The
deliverable -- relative ranking of customer profiles by expected loss for
renewal action -- is exactly what this architecture is designed to produce.
